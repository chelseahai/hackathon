Step 03 CAD / 3D Simulation Integration — Wireframe

03 / CLO 3D Virtual Muslin Toile Simulation

The cuttable DXF file exported from Step 2 is imported into CLO 3D. The four pattern pieces are auto-stitched along their matching seam notches and draped onto a parametric avatar matching the subject's dimensions to inspect drape, seam alignment, and physical fit before cutting any fabric.
